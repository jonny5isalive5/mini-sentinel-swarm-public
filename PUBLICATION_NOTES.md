# Publication scope and preservation

This is a new project-only publication snapshot, prepared on 16 October 2026. It contains no imported Git history, old branches or old tags. The original repositories, full history and evidence remain private and untouched.

Personal workspace paths and private session references have been replaced with neutral aliases. Historical origin links are labelled private. Documentation has been corrected for this publication. Original and public SHA-256s, removals and archive-member transformations are recorded in PUBLICATION_PROVENANCE.json. A changed file is not claimed to have its original hash. Historical hashes inside retained records are not silently resealed.

Research conclusions, failures, numerical outcomes and limitations have not been improved or reinterpreted. Computational source, models, data and executable bytes are checked separately against the private snapshot. No experiments, model calls, builds or training were run to prepare this copy. The only executed verifier reads local files and archive members.

Research snapshots are not independent validation, and retained licences/attribution do not resolve outstanding upstream licensing uncertainty. The publication scan checks specified patterns and reviewed content; it cannot guarantee the absence of every possible unknown secret.
